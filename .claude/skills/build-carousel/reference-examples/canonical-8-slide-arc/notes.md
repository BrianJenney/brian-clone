## Subject

The most common viral carousel shape for a teaching deck: name a problem the viewer recognizes,
promise a fix, hand over the actual asset unpaywalled, explain why the problem happens, explain
what the fix is really changing, prove it with a before/after, raise the stakes, then close on a
comment trigger. The argument is carried by big type, one idea per slide, not by diagrams.

## Italic word chain

`[defining-word] -> fix -> (none) -> [opposing-virtue] -> [unchanged-thing] -> [domain-word] -> [shaper-word] -> [short phrase]`

One italic word per slide except the asset slide, where the kicker carries the emphasis instead.
Each word is the deck's vocabulary, not decoration. Read in order, the chain is a summary of the
argument.

## What makes this work

The asset lands on slide 3, early and ungated, so the viewer gets paid before being asked for
anything. Slides 4 and 5 then explain why the asset works, which converts a screenshot-and-leave
viewer into someone who reads to the end. The comment trigger on slide 8 is for reach and
follow-up, never for access.
